01 / About

A bit about me.

I'm Audrey. I'm a future novelist and New York Times contributor. I'm also a software engineer living in Brooklyn with my husband.
My dream is to manufacture a world that people can lose themselves in.

This little corner of the internet is about writing, and it isn't about writing. It's a personal garden tended to from a lifetime of seeds. It's an homage to my nine-year-old self who ignored multiple deadlines to spend six weeks responding to the homework essay prompt "Tell a story about a class fieldtrip to the moon". It's a series of love letters to every book I've stayed up late reading, to every film or TV show I've carried with me into the next day, and to every online piece of media that's warranted a verbal recap to the people in my life. It's a small reclaiming of space for all the group discussions I've been happy to stay quiet in. It's an earnest attempt to finally, just maybe, find those perfect words that will make it all alright.

If you've ever had a story to tell, this portfolio is dedicated to you.

While this website was generated with the help of Github Copilot, all words published here are and always will be a byproduct of my brain, never AI.
